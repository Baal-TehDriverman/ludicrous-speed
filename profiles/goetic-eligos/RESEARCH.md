# Research Dossier — Duke Eligos

## Scope

This is a historical-text research note for the symbolic Hermes profile `goetic-eligos`. It reports how the supplied project source and two public editions describe this office. It is not evidence that a supernatural entity exists, and it must not be used as ritual, invocation, coercion, or a claim of real authority.

## Canonical Record

- **Order:** 15 of 72
- **Traditional title:** Duke
- **Legions in the project corpus:** 60
- **Element / planet:** Fire / Sun
- **Sephirotic operational focus:** Geburah
- **Form / sigil description in the project corpus:** Goodly knight carrying a lance and banner
- **Traditional office language in the project corpus:** Reveals hidden things; predicts war outcomes; inspires command; rules 60 legions

The supplied `msn_goetia_bestiary.reds` corpus preserves a 72-officer list and this record. Public editions of the Goetia likewise present a ranked spirit catalogue, though names, spellings, classifications, and editorial framing vary across witnesses and editions.[1][2]
