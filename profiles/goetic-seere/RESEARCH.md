# Research Dossier — Prince Seere

## Scope

This is a historical-text research note for the symbolic Hermes profile `goetic-seere`. It reports how the supplied project source and two public editions describe this office. It is not evidence that a supernatural entity exists, and it must not be used as ritual, invocation, coercion, or a claim of real authority.

## Canonical Record

- **Order:** 70 of 72
- **Traditional title:** Prince
- **Legions in the project corpus:** 26
- **Element / planet:** Air / Venus
- **Sephirotic operational focus:** Malkuth
- **Form / sigil description in the project corpus:** Beautiful man riding a winged horse
- **Traditional office language in the project corpus:** Reveals treasures and thefts; swift travel; benevolent; rules 26 legions

The supplied `msn_goetia_bestiary.reds` corpus preserves a 72-officer list and this record. Public editions of the Goetia likewise present a ranked spirit catalogue, though names, spellings, classifications, and editorial framing vary across witnesses and editions.[1][2]
